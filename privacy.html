🎱 RackUp — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 July 2026 · Applies to the RackUp Android app (com.rackup.app)

RackUp is a matchmaking app for billiards players in Greece. This page explains, in plain language, what data the app handles and what your rights are.

What we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address, and password (the password is handled and stored by Google Firebase Authentication — we never see it), plus the skill category you choose.
  • Match posts: the availabilities you post or join — venue, game type, date/time, your display name and skill category, and an optional note you write.

That's all. No location tracking, no contacts, no advertising identifiers, no analytics profiles, no data sold to anyone — ever.

Who can see what

  • Your email address is private: only you can access it.
  • Your display name and skill category appear on the matches you post or join, visible to other signed-in players — that's the point of the app.

Where it's stored

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) on Google Cloud servers in Europe, protected by security rules that enforce the access described above. See Firebase's privacy documentation.

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your profile, and the matches you posted. Matches you had joined keep your display name in their record; email us if you want that removed too.

Without the app: email rackupbilliard@gmail.com from your registered address with the subject "Account deletion request" and we'll delete everything within 30 days.

Your rights (GDPR)

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time at rackupbilliard@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr).

Children

RackUp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with an updated effective date.
