Questions

Is it free?

Yes. RackUp is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Which games does it cover?

Pool: 8-Ball, 9-Ball and 10-Ball. Carom: 3-cushion and 1-cushion. Each post is for one game, because they are played on different tables.

Which cities?

All over Greece, from Athens and Thessaloniki to Crete and the islands. The hall directory is researched by hand and keeps growing.

Is it on iPhone and Android?

Yes, on both, in Greek and English. Sign in with Apple on iPhone, with Google on Android, or with an email address on either.

How do I find an opponent?

Post when and where you want to play, then pick who plays from the players who ask to join. Or see in the feed who is playing tonight and ask to join their table.
