RackUp

Post when and where you want to play. Another player takes the table.

The billiards matchmaking app for Greece. 8-Ball, 9-Ball and 10-Ball players finding opponents for real games at real halls.

Download on theApp Store Get it onGoogle Play

On iPhone and Android, in English and Greek.

What you can do

  • See who is playing tonight

    Every open table in one feed, filtered by game, city, venue and level. Already at the hall? Switch on Play Now and your table goes to the top with a LIVE badge.

  • You pick who plays

    Players ask to join your table and you choose, so nobody gets double-booked. Or post an open challenge for anyone inside your tier range. Every player carries a category from Pro down to E, so you know what you are walking into.

  • Keep score

    Report the result and your opponent confirms it. Then comes your win rate, your streak, your head-to-head with every player you have faced, monthly leaderboards by city and venue, six levels and fourteen badges.

  • Play people who show up

    A verified check after five matches with no no-shows, a reputation built on who actually turns up, and block and report from any match.

  • 66 halls in 26 cities

    A hand-researched directory of Greek billiard halls, with directions in the app. Browse the halls by city, or δες τις αίθουσες στα ελληνικά.

iPhone and Android

Everything above works on both. Sign in with Apple on iPhone, or with Google on Android, or use an email address on either.

A few things are Android only: the venue map, sorting halls by distance, push notifications, adding a match to your calendar, sharing a match, and a profile photo.

Need help?

Want to report a player, or found a hall we are missing? Email rackupbilliard@gmail.com, or read the support page.
